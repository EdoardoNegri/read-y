# Read-y: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 October 2026*

Read-y is an ebook reader for Android developed by Edoardo Negri. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What Read-y collects

Nothing. Read-y does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It has no account system, contains no advertising, and uses no analytics or crash-reporting services. Read-y does not request internet access, so it cannot send data anywhere.

## Your files

Read-y reads only the folder you grant it through Android's folder picker (by default `Documents/Read_y`). It cannot see any other files on your device. Books you open from other apps with "Open with" or "Share" are copied into that folder.

To show your library, Read-y keeps on your device:
- reading positions, favourites, shelf order, the names, authors and covers you give books, and your reader settings
- small cover pictures and book details, kept in the app's private storage so the shelf loads quickly

A copy of your positions and settings is saved as a hidden file (`.backup.json`) inside your Read_y folder, so your library moves with the folder to a new phone. Android's own device backup may also include the app's settings if you have backup turned on; that is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by Read-y.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Read-y deletes everything in its private storage. Your books and the backup file stay in your Read_y folder until you delete them yourself.

## Children

Read-y is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
